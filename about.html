I’m Cory Cuevas, founder of Cuevas Commercial Capital LLC. I spent two decades inside major banks—Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, and Frost Bank—originating and managing commercial real estate and C&I loans before launching my own brokerage in 2025.

At the banks I originated $15–20 million annually in new commitments across healthcare, manufacturing, professional services, and real estate investors. Since going independent, I’ve closed a range of private-money and non-QM transactions and currently manage a strong pipeline of active deals.

I focus exclusively on business-purpose transactions and shop hundreds of private lenders, banks, credit unions, and alternative financiers so my clients get the highest leverage, best rate, and fastest close possible.
